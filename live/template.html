How to run your first experiment
  1. Press Run. The default pulse occurs from 20 to 40 ms.
  2. Click two distant branches to add local probes. Rotation helps separate overlapping branches.
  3. Change Click action to Move injection site, then click a branch and press Inject now.
  4. Choose a bridge or balancer cell from Any cell to inspect its individual response.
  5. Change a parameter, press Apply, and inject again. Use Reset for a comparison from rest.
  6. Export your traces and experiment log before resetting or changing probes.
What the graphs, colors and interventions mean

The plot shows time in ms and voltage relative to rest in mV. All probes share one automatically scaled y axis; small responses may be hidden by larger ones. Solid and dashed curves share probe colors. The graph displays a rolling window, and the table shows the latest values.

Blue means negative voltage, dark teal is near rest, and gold means positive voltage. Colors clip beyond the manually chosen ± range. Identity colors indicate cells, not activity. The real anatomy is static.

Cell and group means are capacitance weighted. A local probe reads one reconstructed compartment. In the point comparison, all locations within a cell necessarily have the same voltage. Balancer voltage is not ciliary beat frequency.

Radius changes both membrane area and internal conduction. Capacitance also changes leak because leak = C / tau. Current per contact is a saturating scale, not a constant delivered current. All contacts share one assumed sign and strength.

Removing feedback disables only bridge-to-ANN contacts. A cut removes one internal cable edge; membrane and synapses remain on both sides. It does not model injury or healing. Selecting another cut restores the previous edge.

Any cell can be stimulated. For ANN cells, the cell-selector shortcut uses its first exported compartment, not an anatomical soma; use a branch click to choose a meaningful location. No missing paths are invented.

Reproducibility and limits

The full 16,601-compartment spatial model runs alongside a 158-cell point model in a background worker. A direct tree solve implements the same passive backward-Euler step and explicit synaptic currents as the Python reference. Simulation time is never advanced by skipping numerical steps; on slower devices the achieved speed may fall below the request. Switching away from the tab pauses the simulation.

CSV contains the retained trace window. JSON includes the source identifier, starting parameters, interventions, probe-set history and retained samples, but not a full-state checkpoint. New probes have no historical samples. An experiment can continue beyond the original 160 ms; this does not validate long-term dynamics.
