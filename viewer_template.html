What does geometry change?

Equal total capacitance, equal injected current, the same measured contacts.

Means are weighted by capacitance. The balancer readout is not ciliary beat frequency. Controls replay precomputed runs; use the Python code to change parameters.

What is assumed?
  • Uniform cable radius 0.25 µm; axial resistivity 150 Ω·cm; leak time 20 ms.
  • All contacts are excitatory in this preset. Actual signs and strengths are unresolved.
  • Bridge and balancer cells each have one electrical compartment.
  • No action potentials, calcium fluorescence, ciliary mechanics or behavioral fitting.
  • The virtual cut removes a cable connection; it does not model wound healing.
